TrueGrade

Free, open-source, local-only Amazon review trust checker

Open any Amazon product page with customer reviews. TrueGrade adds a trust panel above the reviews with a letter grade and the exact reasoning behind it — no black box.

  • Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to any server
  • No account, no sign-in, no tracking
  • Open-source scoring methodology
View the scoring methodology
