# Adversarial validation: "Living" Digital Nomad Visa Status Tracker

Round 2 AGENT_PICK from `ideas/candidates.md`. A static site covering
~50-60 countries' digital-nomad-visa programs, differentiated from
existing resources by continuous re-verification: a visible "last
verified" date per country plus a free weekly automated change-detector
polling official government sources, flagging updates for manual review
(~3-5 hrs/week estimated labor). Monetization: SafetyWing/Genki
nomad-insurance affiliate links, relocation-consultant referral fees,
eventual paid newsletter.

## Why this candidate got extra scrutiny before validation even started

This project's own institutional memory (from a now-deleted prior run)
flagged that the general digital-nomad-visa-comparison concept had
already been examined once, finding WhereToNomad, GlobalNomad.guide,
Passportivity Index, Immigrant Invest Index, and Staywise as close
competitors. The Round 2 discovery agent's proposed differentiator
(continuous freshness, not matching/filtering) was different enough to
be worth a fresh look — but the adversarial validation pass was
explicitly instructed to check, as its single most important question,
whether any of those competitors (or others) already do visible
freshness dating. They do.

## Adversarial research pass (2026-09-19)

1. **The make-or-break question, answered badly for this candidate:**
   visible "last updated/verified" dating is not a gap in this niche as
   of September 2026 — it is now standard SEO-trust boilerplate across
   the competitive set, several with dates newer than this candidate's
   own weekly cadence would produce:
   - Discovery Sessions: "Last verified: September 17, 2026" — two days
     before this validation.
   - NomadQualify: "Last updated July 21, 2026."
   - WhereNext (getwherenext.com): two separate articles dated
     2026-02-28 and 2026-08-21.
   - VisaDB.io: "last updated 02 April, 2026."
   - Enomads: "last verified June 2026," 125-country coverage.
   - Immigrant Invest and Passportivity: both publish annually-versioned
     2026 indices (55 and 48 countries) with explicit "as of Q4
     2025/2026" methodology language.
   The candidate's entire pitch — "existing resources are static, ours
   visibly isn't" — is factually wrong as a market description right
   now. Whether any of these run an actual automated government-source
   poller (vs. a manually-refreshed page date) couldn't be confirmed due
   to an egress restriction, but that distinction is commercially moot:
   a visitor can only see the date stamp either way, and that stamp is
   already everywhere.
2. **Freshness/tracking positioning is already a recognized
   sub-category competitors market against each other on** — Staywise
   publishes its own "Digital Nomad Visa Tracking Tools Compared" post,
   meaning the "who tracks this best" angle is already a live comparison
   competitors themselves are fighting over, not open ground.
3. **The maintenance-cadence premise is weaker than pitched.** Income
   thresholds do change, but on predictable annual government calendars
   (Portugal's D8, Spain's digital-nomad visa, Colombia's threshold —
   all revised via dated annual decrees, typically January). A
   disciplined once-a-year pass timed to decree dates captures most of
   the real value; continuous weekly polling mainly catches lower-
   frequency irregular events (program suspensions, portal changes).
   3-5 hrs/week across 50-60 countries works out to ~3-6 minutes/country/
   week — thin for genuine re-verification, and the "living, continuous"
   framing oversells what the labor budget can actually deliver.
4. **Distribution: no wedge found.** Beyond the five originally-named
   competitors, at least nine more actively-publishing 2026 nomad-visa
   resources exist (VisaDB, Enomads, WhereNext, Discovery Sessions,
   NomadQualify, Deel, GlobalCitizenSolutions, TheVisaIndex, Peak,
   NestPaths, GlobalWealthProtection, RelocateMe, TaxesForExpats). No
   evidence of a community specifically demanding freshness over the
   matching/filtering functionality several competitors (GlobalNomad.guide
   with 100+ indicators/13 categories, WhereToNomad, Passportivity)
   already provide free.
5. **Monetization is real but thin.** SafetyWing pays ~10% recurring for
   up to ~12 months (roughly a 364-day cookie), ~$4.50/mo per referral on
   a ~$45/mo product — meaningful only at real volume. No indie-hacker
   revenue reports found for a pure nomad-visa-content-plus-insurance-
   affiliate site, itself a negative signal.
6. **Erosion risk is high and fast.** Nomad List is a lean 1-3 person
   operation but has an established audience and could add a "last
   verified" badge plus a cron checker in an afternoon — it's a UI/
   process feature, not deep technology. Any incumbent that already
   date-stamps (VisaDB, NomadQualify) could close whatever residual gap
   exists immediately.

## Ten-question check

1. **Why hasn't this been captured already?** It has — freshness/
   tracking-tool positioning is already a recognized, actively-marketed
   sub-category in this niche (Staywise's own comparison post proves
   competitors are already fighting over exactly this angle).
2. **What prevents a competitor from copying us?** Nothing — adding a
   "last verified" date and a change-detector is a process/UI feature a
   lean existing operator (Nomad List) could ship in an afternoon.
3. **Why would someone switch from what they use now?** No credible
   answer — several existing resources already show dates as current or
   more current than this candidate's own cadence would produce.
4. **Where do the first 100 users come from?** No wedge found in an
   already 14+-competitor field.
5. **Where do the first 1,000 come from?** No basis found.
6. **Who pays?** Nomad-insurance affiliate conversions, in theory — but
   no revenue precedent found for this exact content-plus-affiliate
   model.
7. **Why would they pay (click through/convert) vs. free alternatives?**
   No differentiation identified once the freshness claim is neutralized.
8. **What can we offer that competitors cannot easily reproduce?**
   Nothing identified.
9. **Can we realistically acquire customers at €0?** No clear channel
   found in a crowded, already-SEO-optimized field.
10. **Is there real demand evidence?** The underlying pain (visa program
    data going stale, programs disappearing without notice) is real, but
    it is already being addressed, visibly, by more competitors than the
    discovery pass originally found — this isn't unmet demand, it's a
    contested niche the discovery agent under-surveyed.

## Agent Recommendation: KILL

The differentiator this candidate was built on — "existing resources are
static, ours is visibly and continuously current" — is factually false as
a market description in September 2026. At least six named competitors
already display freshness dates, one of them two days more recent than
this validation itself. The underlying pain is real, but it's already a
contested battleground, not unclaimed ground, and the proposed ~3-5
hrs/week maintenance budget is thin relative to genuine continuous
re-verification across 50-60 countries. This is the second time in this
project's history that a "the incumbents are static, we'll be dynamic/
automatic" wedge has looked open at discovery time and collapsed under a
dedicated adversarial re-check — worth treating as a standing pattern to
watch for, not a coincidence.
