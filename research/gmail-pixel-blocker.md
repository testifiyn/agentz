  smaller slice than the browsing public.

  despite years of operation — the same negative signal found in the

  changes frequently; keeping a content-script-based blocker working is
  recurring, unbounded work, not a one-time build — inconsistent with a
  low-effort €0 MVP that should stay maintainable without dedicated
  ongoing engineering time.
