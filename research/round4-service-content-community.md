# Round 4 — Business-model pivot: service / content / community axes

**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Trigger:** MEMORY.md's standing recommendation #2 after 11/11 software-product
discovery strategies failed (every software idea gets AI-cloned within 1-3
weeks regardless of complexity). This round deliberately tested business
models where the moat is a human relationship, curation judgment, or
network trust rather than a clonable software mechanism.

Three parallel tracks, each required to find real evidence (existing paid
gigs/subscriptions/competitors, forum/Reddit complaints, pricing precedent)
before calling anything a survivor, and to actively search for why a
candidate might already be captured.

---

## Track A — Productized "done for you" service (SMB/solo-professional)

5 niches screened: e-commerce chargeback/dispute management (KILL — already
dominated by fully-automated AI SaaS: Chargeflow, Justt, Chargeback Gurus),
SAM.gov registration bundling for govcon newcomers (WEAK — one-time
transaction, Fiverr-gig-mill saturated), RFP/bid-proposal writing for govcon
subcontractors (MARGINAL — bracketed by funded AI-native tools GovEagle/
pWin.ai), small-importer tariff/HTS classification advisory (KILLED — same
liability-driven "wants a licensed professional, not a solo tool" pattern
that killed the earlier DAC8 crypto-tax candidate), and **Google Business
Profile suspension/reinstatement appeal-writing (SURVIVOR, marginal)**.

**GBP reinstatement/appeal-writing — the Round 4 survivor:**
- Mass, unexplained GBP suspension wave hit "thousands" of established,
  well-reviewed listings starting ~27 Apr 2026, ongoing.
- Real existing spend: boutique operators charge $250-800/case, several on
  pay-on-success terms ($0 upfront, paid only if reinstated) — proves
  willingness to pay for an outcome, not just a theoretical need.
- Genuine judgment requirement: Google's appeal process is a manual,
  non-API, frequently-changing human review queue; ~60% of DIY appeals fail
  on technical/narrative grounds, not because the business did anything
  wrong. This resists full software automation, unlike the prior 11
  software candidates.
- Natural retainer upsell: some businesses get re-suspended days after
  reinstatement, creating demand for ongoing "profile hygiene monitoring."
- Named competitors are established (years-old) boutique local-SEO shops
  (Whitespark, SterlingSky, GBP Suspensions, SteadyDemand, Searchscope) —
  not an overnight flood of AI-agency clones. The saturation-kill signal
  this project watches for was not found.
- Concrete €0 first-10-customers channels: r/SEO, r/LocalSEO,
  r/smallbusiness (suspension complaints appear in real time),
  localsearchforum.com (active populated community), local-business
  Facebook groups, direct outreach to businesses with visibly vanished Maps
  listings.
- **Flagged fatal risks (unresolved):** Google already shipped a more
  structured self-serve appeals tool in Feb 2024 and could keep closing the
  DIY gap (same "platform fixes its own problem" pattern that killed the
  nomad-visa-tracker candidate); incumbents have a decade of domain
  authority; pay-on-success norms mean 3-30 day cash-flow lag with no
  guaranteed close; monitoring-retainer upsell is unproven.

## Track B — Paid niche newsletter / intelligence briefing

5 niches screened, **0 survivors**: shadow-fleet tanker/sanctions
intelligence (KILL — moat is satellite/AIS data infrastructure, not
curation), business-jet market intelligence (KILL — already captured by a
paid Substack, no €0-accessible audience community), HOA/condo-insurance
compliance briefing (KILL — best raw audience signal, 59k-member r/HOA, but
every existing "newsletter" in the space is free, given away as a loss
leader by law firms and the trade association — structurally crowds out a
reader-paid model), semiconductor export-control compliance-ops briefing
(MARGINAL, not picked — bracketed by SemiAnalysis, 180k paid subs, and
World ECR, both with resources to contest any wedge), independent P&C
agent hard-market intelligence (KILL — Insurance Journal gives the exact
target audience free subscriptions, ad-funded, no incentive to pay).

Structural read: in 2/5 cases the specific, direct kill evidence was that
the target audience already receives equivalent content free from a
non-reader-funded incentivized source (law firm, trade association,
advertiser) — a sharper and more specific version of "looks open until
checked closely."

## Track C — Community-driven matching / curated marketplace

5 pairs screened, **0 clean survivors**: semi-retired tradespeople ↔
homeowners for small jobs (MARGINAL, least-bad — sharp documented pain,
including an FTC consent order against Angi for the lead-fee model's
small-job failure mode, and fits the €0 concierge-MVP playbook, but
TaskRabbit/Thumbtack/Angi/Handyman Connection/free Nextdoor-Facebook
collectively already cover the ground imperfectly), specialized
respite/companion care for families of autistic children (KILLED —
CareYaya already runs this exact free, funded model at scale), durable
medical equipment reuse matching (KILLED — mature nonprofit ecosystem
already serves it), retired engineers/machinists mentoring or overflow
labor (KILLED/weak — SCORE already covers mentoring free; contingent labor
is a commoditized staffing-agency space), diaspora home-cooked meals
(not pursued — Shef.com's 2026 retrenchment signals this is an
operations-heavy logistics business, not a matching play).

## Round 4 overall verdict

Of 15 niches screened across 3 non-software business-model axes, exactly
**one marginal survivor** (GBP suspension/reinstatement), explicitly
flagged by its own screening agent as not a clean/high-confidence pick.
The pivot away from pure software did surface one genuinely different
candidate (its moat sits inside Google's opaque manual review process, not
in any software this project could build or that a clone could copy
overnight) — but the dominant pattern from the 11 software rounds
partially repeated here too: most "looks open" niches, once checked
directly, turned out to already have an incumbent (platform, nonprofit,
trade association, or funded startup) covering the ground, just imperfectly.

**Decision: do not run a 4th discovery round yet.** Per the project's
discovery-reopening rule, reopening search is for when a candidate is
killed and too few credible candidates remain. Here we have exactly one
candidate, it is time-sensitive (the suspension wave is already 6 months
old), and further search would cost real time against a decaying window
without first spending cheap effort finding out whether this one holds up.
Next action: dedicated adversarial validation of the GBP-reinstatement
candidate specifically (re-check current competitor count/maturity, verify
Google hasn't further upgraded self-serve appeals since April 2026, and
sanity-check realistic close rates), per the screening agent's own
recommendation.
